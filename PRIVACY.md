# Privacy Policy — ROBO Recovery

ROBO Recovery runs **100% locally** on your Windows PC.

- **No telemetry or analytics:** ROBO Recovery does not collect, log, or upload your files, filenames, scan results, or personal data.
- **No external network calls unless you trigger them:**
  - Clicking **Download Android Tools (adb)** downloads Google's official `platform-tools-latest-windows.zip` from `dl.google.com`.
  - Clicking **Check for Updates**, **Donate**, or **Feedback** opens your chosen link or checks the release version.
- **Local settings:** UI layout, theme, language, and custom signatures are stored locally in `%LocalAppData%\ROBO Recovery\`.
